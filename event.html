Featured Activities

  1. Highs, Lows & Buffers (Icebreaker & Check-in)
  2. My Support Network Tree (Visual Mapping)
  3. Boundary Roleplay Cards (Skill-Building)
  4. Coping Box Strategy Exchange (Resource Sharing)
  5. Goal Chunking Ladder (Action Planning)

Attendee Tips

  • Arrival Time: Plan to sign in or join the virtual room 5–10 minutes early to resolve any technical issues and ensure a prompt start.
  • Admission & Registration: Have your registration confirmation details ready prior to the event, as spots for training sessions may be limited or require verified access.
  • Preparation & Participation: Bring a notebook or digital device for taking notes, and come prepared to engage in discussions or interactive exercises.
  • Materials & Follow-up: Keep track of contact hours and any downloadable resources provided during the session for your records or professional development.
